This paper studies differentially private stochastic optimization for (multi-layer) fully connected neural networks with a single output node.

- For generalized linear models with monotone, Lipschitz link functions, we construct a convex surrogate loss that upper-bounds the generalization error, and use objective perturbation to obtain a private ERM algorithm whose excess risk, $O(\log^2(n)/\sqrt{n})$, does not depend on the data dimension. We also treat the ReLU link and contrast well-specified and misspecified models.
- We extend these ideas to two-layer networks with sigmoid or ReLU activations.
- Using Neural Tangent Kernel analysis, we give the first excess population-risk bound for DP-SGD (Abadi et al., 2016) on fully connected networks of arbitrary depth when both the sample size and the width are large, and discuss how DP-SGD's parameters affect utility, theoretically and empirically.
